* Purpose

This is the living plan and completion record for moving Moppe's production
renderer toward the visual target below.  It is deliberately about the actual
game path, not the Atelier.  A task becomes DONE only after it is present in a
gameplay or gazetteer capture and survives the performance gate.

[[file:../ideal-dream-gfx.png][Ideal dream graphics concept, 2026-08-05]]

The reference is not primarily a request for Nanite.  Its strongest cues are
composition, directional light, readable shadow hierarchy, varied conifer
silhouettes, dense multi-scale ground cover, terrain material structure, and
depth from atmosphere.  Geometry streaming and mesh shaders matter because
they let those cues remain dense and stable, not because they create the look
by themselves.

* Visual contract

- The rider and route remain the focus; the chase lens does not flatten the
  entire landscape into a wide-angle panorama.
- Nearby trees read as living organisms: trunk, tiered boughs, smaller sprays,
  gaps, asymmetry, age variation, warm transmitted light, and cool interior
  shade.
- Forests make broad pools of shade, while local boughs, grass, rocks, and the
  rider make sharper contact shadows.
- Distant trees retain a calm, coherent silhouette.  LOD changes must not
  crawl, shimmer, or form obvious distance rings.
- The ground is a layered habitat rather than one grass primitive repeated at
  even spacing: grass heights, herbs, flowers, ferns, litter, stones, bare
  trail, and moisture all occupy different ecological niches.
- Mountains have readable large-scale geology and snow support.  Haze
  separates ridges without bleaching the whole image.
- Sun, sky, clouds, fog, materials, and exposure agree on one time of day.

Keep the camera and world recipe fixed so comparisons mean something.

*** DONE GFX-011 Cinematic chase framing

Replace the current 100--109 degree gameplay lens with a typed, narrower chase

Acceptance: the rider reads larger, trees retain height, and the route still
offers enough peripheral information at speed.

** Light and shade

*** VERIFY GFX-020 Camera-local sun shadow level

Render a texel-stable, camera-local orthographic sun map every frame.  Draw
only the nearby periodic terrain chunks and nearest periodic image of each
tree.  This replaces the low-density whole-world map in the production Metal
path; retain an explicit future seam for a far terrain level if captures prove
it necessary.

Acceptance: nearby trunks and crowns visibly anchor to the ground; shadow
resolution follows the player; CPU submission and forest candidate counts are
bounded by the local region.

*** TODO GFX-021 Temporal ground-truth ambient occlusion

Add GTAO from reversed-Z depth and normals, temporally accumulated with motion
vectors.  Apply it mainly to indirect light, never as a grey full-image grade.

*** TODO GFX-022 Foliage light transport

Give leaf material a two-sided diffuse/transmission lobe, sun-facing edge
warmth, sky exposure, and restrained specular variation.  Keep tree interiors
legible without making every crown uniformly emissive.

** Tree form and continuity

*** VERIFY GFX-030 Conifer bough assemblies

Replace the near conifer tier's single lobed volume with an assembly of radial
boughs and needle sprays.  Keep it opaque geometry, under the existing bounded
meshlet budget, with deterministic asymmetry from the individual's seed.

*** TODO GFX-031 Continuous projected-detail transitions

Stagger thresholds per individual and grow/fade newly admitted organs using a
stable screen-space transition.  Add a calm far representation only after the
near and middle tree are convincing.

*** TODO GFX-032 Species and life-history grammar

Author several conifer architectures and broadleaf crowns.  Drive height,
crown ratio, trunk taper, dead lower branches, lean, hue, and spacing from age
and habitat instead of uniform independent noise.

** Habitat floor

*** TODO GFX-040 Multi-species undergrowth assemblies

Extend the undergrowth mesh-shader population from grass alone to bounded
assemblies for bunch grass, fern, low herb, flower, seed head, litter, and
small stone.  Keep projected-size rejection and stable individual decisions.

Landed so far: the family table in undergrowth.metal — fern rosettes
assembled from re-rooted shoots around a damp-shade crown lattice, and
flowering forbs in single-species drifts whose colour also washes the
terrain substrate (grass_medium.h), with head chroma collapsing at range.
Remaining: seed heads, litter, small stones.

*** TODO GFX-041 Trail and clearing composition

Make the route a material and population event: compressed dirt, loose stone,
edge flowers, lower cover, and occasional roots.  Preserve gameplay clearance.

** Terrain, atmosphere, and finishing

*** TODO GFX-050 Material-scale terrain detail

Add macro albedo/roughness breakup, slope-aligned rock, soil exposure, and
actual scattered rock assemblies without turning the terrain into texture
noise.

*** TODO GFX-051 Layered aerial perspective

Replace one uniform distance wash with height-aware aerial perspective and a
low-frequency valley component.  Evaluate silhouettes against three or more
ridge depths.

*** TODO GFX-052 Volumetric cloud and sun integration

Give clouds light-facing volume, self-shadow, and agreement with ground
illumination.  Keep the existing physical visibility signal as the sun/flare
authority.

*** TODO GFX-060 Performance and quality tiers

Measure the target shot at low, balanced, and high.  Track terrain shadow
draws, forest candidates, emitted meshlets, undergrowth instances, GPU pass
times, temporal stability, and memory.  Quality tiers should change density
and reach, not silently substitute a different visual language.

* Verification protocol

1. Build the production macOS app and run unit tests.
2. Capture the same target viewpoint before and after whenever possible.
3. Inspect full-resolution crops of a hero tree, its ground contact, a middle
   forest, and the distant tree line.
4. Move slowly through the scene and inspect LOD transitions, TAA breakup,
   shadow swimming, and wind motion; a still image alone cannot verify them.
5. Record GPU pass timing and emitted/candidate counters before increasing
   density.
6. Update this file with what actually shipped, what the capture showed, and
   the next highest visual bottleneck.

* Work log

** 2026-08-05

- Created this campaign record from the supplied concept mockup and the
  production screenshots.
- Confirmed that the first tranche must couple camera composition, local
  shadow density, and near-tree silhouette rather than treating mesh shaders
  as a standalone visual feature.
- Changed chase/front gameplay framing from 100--109 degrees to a typed
  78--86 degree range.  Helmet, cinematic, gazetteer, and inspection cameras
  retain authored lenses.  Unit tests cover the chase endpoints and helmet
  boundary.
- Added a 2048-pixel, 160-metre-radius, 48-metre look-ahead sun-shadow level.
  Its light-space origin advances in texel increments.  It submits only nearby
  native terrain chunks and one nearest periodic image of each tree, and has a
  distinct =shadow= GPU timing column.
- Replaced each detailed conifer tier's sealed lobed volume with five explicit
  seeded bough sprays.  They widen, droop, and turn upward, leaving real light
  gaps while remaining within the existing 32-vertex/60-primitive meshlet
  bounds.  Per-tree projected-detail thresholds are now stably staggered.
- Built the macOS production app, compiled the Metal library, passed all unit
  tests, and captured =/tmp/moppe-dream-tranche.png= plus the four-view
  =/tmp/moppe-dream-survey/terrain-survey.png= survey.  The gameplay capture
  ran at 2560x1600 drawable / 1920x1200 scene and displayed 57 FPS, but that is
  an observational capture rather than the isolated performance gate.
- Capture verdict: composition and ground anchoring are materially better;
  forest shadow pools are now obvious and coherent.  Conifer whorls admit
  light and read as branches, but remain too sparse and regular.  Faceted
  broadleaf crown blobs are now the most conspicuous tree-form defect.  Keep
  GFX-020 and GFX-030 in VERIFY until a moving stability pass and isolated GPU
  timing are recorded.
