* Purpose

This is the living plan and completion record for moving Moppe's production
renderer toward the visual target below.  It is deliberately about the actual
game path, not the Atelier.  A task becomes DONE only after it is present in a
gameplay or gazetteer capture and survives the performance gate.

[[file:../ideal-dream-gfx.png][Ideal dream graphics concept, 2026-08-05]]

The reference is not primarily a request for Nanite.  Its strongest cues are
composition, directional light, readable shadow hierarchy, varied conifer
silhouettes, dense multi-scale ground cover, terrain material structure, and
depth from atmosphere.  Geometry streaming and mesh shaders matter because
they let those cues remain dense and stable, not because they create the look
by themselves.

* Visual contract

- The rider and route remain the focus; the chase lens does not flatten the
  entire landscape into a wide-angle panorama.
- Nearby trees read as living organisms: trunk, tiered boughs, smaller sprays,
  gaps, asymmetry, age variation, warm transmitted light, and cool interior
  shade.
- Forests make broad pools of shade, while local boughs, grass, rocks, and the
  rider make sharper contact shadows.
- Distant trees retain a calm, coherent silhouette.  LOD changes must not
  crawl, shimmer, or form obvious distance rings.
- The ground is a layered habitat rather than one grass primitive repeated at
  even spacing: grass heights, herbs, flowers, ferns, litter, stones, bare
  trail, and moisture all occupy different ecological niches.
- Mountains have readable large-scale geology and snow support.  Haze
  separates ridges without bleaching the whole image.
- Sun, sky, clouds, fog, materials, and exposure agree on one time of day.

*** VERIFY GFX-042 Continuous grass medium through the far register

The far substrate and middle density column now evaluate one bounded optical
response, one world-space grain cascade, and the same broad gust phase. The
missing middle rung is no longer addressed by widened tufts or an opaque top
sheet. A conservative terrain-following envelope enters a four-sample density
column; its finite view path preserves vertical extent without letting a
grazing ray become an infinite dark slab. Its dispatch patches share
world-space edge evaluations, so it is one field rather than a second plant
population.

This is a Gate 3 and Gate 4 proof, not completion. Retiring blades now converge
their own tint, sky/direct moments, transmission, and glint in place; the
column owns aggregate coverage and texture. Calibrate the profile in longer
motion, prove rootability and the habitat edge from a low aerial pass, and
price the full scene distribution. Texture-energy and motion residuals locate
a discontinuity; they do not decide whether the result feels like one world.

*** VERIFY GFX-042b The representation frontier, stated honestly

The canonical scene -- grass lab plain, chase height, sunward:
MOPPE_GRASS_LAB=1 MOPPE_UPLIFT_YEARS=0 MOPPE_TERRAIN_PROFILE=smoke
MOPPE_LOOK="1718.75 54.7 39.06 1790 50.5 108 68" -- shows a straight
boundary where blade geometry ends and a flat band runs to the horizon.
Every far-grass claim must include THIS frame at low and high scene
resolution, plus cross-lit, downward, and aerial companions.
It isolated two defects now addressed structurally. (1) The main dark-register
mismatch was color space: hardware delivered linear texture samples while the
far path divided by a display-space mean. Both unresolved evaluations now use
the measured linear mean. (2) Structural absence required a finite vertical
density path, not a color correction. The first attempt raised the terrain
itself; this violated physics and made the rendered ground camera-dependent,
so it remains removed. The current proof is a density column above the true
terrain. It must still be judged as a continuous field, never rewarded for
adding a few coarse plants to the far band.

*** VERIFY GFX-044 Ensemble-limit contract for the forest/terrain hand-off

docs/ensemble-continuity.md predicts the forest tint band carries the
same zeroth-moment bug the sward escaped: terrain colours forested
ground with a separately authored resemblance (ground_value *
(0.48, 0.72, 0.34)) rather than the crown shader's own ensemble limit.
Implemented as =moppe_forest_distribution_light=: retiring crown fragments and

this VERIFY until varied sun angles and longer player-controlled flight have
tested the same contract.

*** TODO GFX-045 Calibrate coarse rungs from measured fine-rung statistics

Graduate the instruments from verification to calibration: fit the

*** VERIFY GFX-046 Nested population hierarchy for distant forest

The four-stratum stand volume now has a real spatial quotient rather than one
fixed tessellation at every distance. A 24 m world patch has thirty-six 4 m
children and nine exactly nested 8 m parents. Projected cell width selects the
partition: child only above 3.5 scene pixels, both complete partitions from
3.5 to 2 pixels, parent only below 2. During the overlap they divide one
optical-depth claim before extinction and sample explicit density/moment mips;
cells never retire independently. Stable input-grid compaction within each
object group and one coalesced meshlet per patch/height-stratum keep local blend
order and work topology quiet in motion.

The rejected first version also added a 16 m parent. At the current 2.4 km
reach the 8 m carrier remains about 2.4 pixels wide, so that rung was premature
and made the benchmark worse. The accepted 4-to-8 tree measures 11.1703 ms
median and 14.6704 ms p95 for the forest block, versus 11.0599 / 14.3039 ms for
the fixed 4 m lattice. A 120-frame 42 m/s bloom-free aerial translation shows
no event spike through 2.6 km, and a 90-frame actual forest ride shows no
camera ring or restructuring. Keep VERIFY until longer player-controlled
flight and varied resolutions exercise the same projected-error law. Extend
the tree only when projected support demands it; do not add rungs to make the
system look more sophisticated.

*** VERIFY GFX-043 Frame instrumentation for distance rendering

First slice landed: tools/frame-texture-profile maps grass-gradient
image rows to ground distance through the gazetteer camera pose and
plots per-band |dI/dx|, Laplacian, and anisotropy across builds, with
floor-merge and mid-hold scalars. Its calibration run over this
session's build history immediately taught three things. (1) It
rediscovered the pre-fix pop-in as an energy PEAK at 25-30 m backlit --
and thereby exposed that raw energy measures contrast, not quality: the
artifact outscored the fix, so scalars locate change while the overlaid
curves decide its sign. (2) The coverage-conserving tuft band removed
that peak and extended floor-merge from ~85 m to 110 m+. (3) The
dominant remaining far artifact is a sharp cliff at the tile-window edge
in front-lit frames, which GFX-042's substrate nap must dissolve (or the
tuft band must fade approaching the window). Second slice landed: MOPPE_CAPTURE_AUX=1 makes every capture also dump
the scene-resolution motion texture and depth buffer with a matrix
sidecar, and frame-texture-profile --ride warps consecutive frames
through the renderer's own vectors — the residual is change motion
cannot explain, binned by true depth. Third slice: MOPPE_GLIDE=<shot>
turns the gazetteer into the isolated moving-camera instrument — the
named shot's camera translates along its heading, every frame captured,
no vehicle, no HUD, no particles, frozen wind (MOPPE_GLIDE_FRAMES,
MOPPE_GLIDE_SPEED).

The isolation flipped a conclusion. A first "measurement" through a
demo ride (bike, HUD popups, dust, trail dirt, sun glare, and a
half-compensated every-2nd-frame stride) showed a confident 2.5x
appearance-residual wall at the vegetation window edge. The clean lab
glide at 12 m/s shows NO wall: smooth monotone decay through the tuft
band and across the window edge (~2e-3 there, ~15x below near field).
Grow-in transitions plus exposure settling really do make arrivals
quiet at that speed. Open questions for GFX-042's criterion: play-speed
glides (3x frontier crossing rate), drift-crossing headings, and the
real world rather than the lab plain. Remaining tooling: approximate
depth reprojection for arbitrary gazetteer frames.

Repeated failure mode: static captures pass inspection while play
immediately reveals blatant mid/far-field vegetation absence.  Judging
distance rendering from full frames by impression does not work — the
missing thing is precisely what an eye skims past in a still.  Build the
instrument: a frame-analysis tool (e.g. tools/frame-texture-profile)
that turns a gazetteer frame into per-distance-band statistics —
high-frequency (Laplacian) energy, vertical-gradient energy (the
verticality signature of grass), green-channel texture energy — and
plots them against distance.  The grass-gradient frames map image rows
to ground distance monotonically, so a per-row profile needs no depth;
arbitrary frames can reproject approximate depth from the gazetteer CSV
camera pose plus terrain-elevation.f32.  Every claim about distance
vegetation then ships as a profile curve compared across builds: a
representation that dies early is a cliff in the curve, visible without
any eye at all.  Extend to consecutive ride frames (temporal energy per
band) for motion claims.

* Verification protocol

1. Build the production macOS app and run unit tests.
2. Capture the same target viewpoint before and after whenever possible.
3. Inspect full-resolution crops of a hero tree, its ground contact, a middle
   forest, and the distant tree line.
4. Move slowly through the scene and inspect LOD transitions, TAA breakup,
   shadow swimming, and wind motion; a still image alone cannot verify them.
5. Record GPU pass timing and emitted/candidate counters before increasing
   density.
6. Update this file with what actually shipped, what the capture showed, and
   the next highest visual bottleneck.
