A small open world to walk, ride, fly, fish, and camp in, grown from its geology.

Two million years before the trailhead

The world begins as a low plain with a few shallow seas. The crust lifts it, fastest where the mountains will be, for the first half million years.

What you see behind these words is that simulation, the one that made the game's world, on a lattice an eighth as fine, running on your own computer.

Every step of fifty thousand years, each stream cuts its bed by the water it gathers.

It carries what it cut downhill and lays it down where it slows. The valleys branch and deepen, the ridges sharpen between them, and the slopes creep. Then the forest settles where the ground will have it, and the rivers find their beds.

Still the same world

Down there, at the trailhead, a bike is parked by the cairn.

  • Walk the fells, or ride the Trail 90 down the trail.
  • Glide off a ridge with the bike slung below the wing.
  • Take the skiff out and cast a float from it.
  • Pick mushrooms, light a fire, and fry them.
  • Hang the hammock between two birches and watch the stars.
