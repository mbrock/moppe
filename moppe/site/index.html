Two million years before the trailhead

The world begins as a low plain with a few shallow seas. The crust lifts it, fastest where the mountains will be, for the first half million years.

Rain falls on it all the while. Every step of fifty thousand years, each stream cuts its bed by the water it gathers, carries what it cut downhill, and lays it down where it slows. The valleys branch and deepen, the ridges sharpen between them, and the slopes creep.

What you are watching is the simulation that made the game's world, on a lattice an eighth as fine, running on your own computer.
