# Schedule finite uplift inside deep-time evolution

## Outcome

Uplift has an explicit physical duration independent of the total landscape
evolution duration. Play can build relief for part of two million years and
then relax without continued tectonic forcing.

## Scope

- Add a typed uplift duration to `StreamPowerEvolution`.
- Integrate only the overlap between each geological step and the active
  uplift interval.
- Give every generation profile an explicit schedule; begin Play at 500,000
  years of uplift inside its two-million-year evolution.
- Invalidate finished worlds made with the old forcing.

No channel, hillslope, capacity, or deposition-width law changes in this item.

## Acceptance

- Integrated uplift is exact when the uplift boundary falls inside a step.
- Changing the geological time step does not change total scheduled uplift.
- Zero-duration and no-uplift worlds remain valid.
- Recipe tests state the profile schedules.
- The optimized same-seed Play world completes and its replacement cache hits.

## Evidence

- `StreamPowerEvolution::uplift_duration` is typed in Julian years. Every
  step integrates the clamped overlap between its interval and the active
  forcing interval; incision, sediment routing, deposition, and diffusion
  continue for the full evolution duration.
- `finite_uplift_integrates_the_exact_active_interval` gets the same one-metre
  rise and 200 m3 tectonic ledger from 300-year and 128-year steps when the
  500-year forcing boundary falls inside a step.
- `zero_uplift_duration_disables_tectonic_forcing` proves that an explicit
  no-uplift world is valid.
- Smoke, Fast, Play, and Research state explicit uplift durations in recipe
  tests. Play evolves for 2 My with uplift during its first 500 ky.
- Cache format version 4 rejects finished worlds generated by continuous
  uplift.
- An optimized seed-123 Play cache miss completed and saved the replacement
  world. A twelve-view follow-up launch loaded that exact local cache. Its
  rendered route has broad forested ridges and rolling horizons instead of
  all-world serrated mountains; the remaining fine fluting is carried into
  TER-002 rather than tuned here.
