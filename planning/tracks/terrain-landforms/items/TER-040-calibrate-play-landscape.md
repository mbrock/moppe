# Calibrate and accept the Play landscape

## Outcome

One parameter set becomes the Play recipe because it passes the RFC landscape
criteria across the accepted seed suite and is good to ride, not because one
cinematic angle looks impressive.

## Acceptance

- The seed suite covers varied coasts, lakes, relief, and trail conditions.
- Physical summaries and conservation budgets are archived with the verdict.
- Gazetteer and water captures show mountains, rolling land, branching
  drainage, widening trunk valleys, depositional lowlands, and no raster-scale
  pathologies.
- Trails remain complete and rideable.
- An optimized fresh generation completes, saves cache version identity, and
  the following launch is a cache hit.
